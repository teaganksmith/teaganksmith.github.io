Evolutionary Genomics of C4 Photosynthesis

Sage Lab, Department of Ecology & Evolutionary Biology, University of Toronto · Toronto, ON · May – September 2020 · Supervisor: Dr. Tammy Sage · NSERC Undergraduate Student Research Award recipient

My Role & Funding

Funded by the NSERC (National Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada) Undergraduate Student Research Award, I conducted an independent literature review on C4 photosynthesis, covering convergent evolution, leaf anatomy, gene expression, and the anthropogenic significance of C4 crop research. I also interviewed researchers in the field and compiled findings into reports presented at lab meetings.

Research Focus

C4 photosynthesis has evolved independently across roughly 66 angiosperm lineages, making it one of the strongest examples of convergent evolution in biology. This review examined the physiological and genomic mechanisms driving that convergence, from Kranz anatomy and plasmodesmata function to lateral gene transfer and transcriptional regulation, and their implications for bioengineering climate-resilient crops.
