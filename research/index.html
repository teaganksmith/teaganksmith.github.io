Passive Acoustic Monitoring · 2026

Seconds-Scale Social Contagion in Nocturnal Flight Calls: A Calibrated Bound from Passive Acoustic Monitoring

A pre-registered point-process test for call-and-response behaviour in 35,402 expert Raven annotations from six autonomous sensors. The pre-registered test rejected its null in every sensor; a bandwidth diagnostic showed that rejection was a property of the null model. Two independent implementations were then adjudicated against series with known ground truth, and disagreed by more than the effect they were built to measure.

Complete Python Raven Point-Process Statistics

Bioacoustics · 2026

Categorical Structure in a Sperm Whale Coda Repertoire: A Replication with Transparent Methods

A replication of a 2024 Nature Communications result over Project CETI's 1,501-coda Dominica corpus, using deliberately simple methods. Of ten click-count classes, only one shows categorical structure that two independent tests agree on, and the most statistically striking result of the run was disqualified on inspection as three outlier files.

Complete Python Signal Processing Unsupervised Clustering

Spatial Ecology · Summer 2026

Sperm Whale Foraging Habitat and Deep-Sea Mining Acoustic Risk, North Atlantic

Species distribution modelling of sperm whale foraging habitat from 71,104 GBIF occurrence records, overlaid with ISA deep-sea mining exploration contracts and published acoustic impact zones. An adversarial verification pass overturned the project's original headline finding, which is documented rather than quietly corrected.

Complete Python Google Earth Engine QGIS

Fin Whale Scientific Visualization — EDMAKTUB

Producing infographic and poster visuals for the EDMAKTUB Fin Whale Project, depicting distribution, movement, and migration of fin whales (Balaenoptera physalus) along the Catalan coast. Developed in collaboration with the research team at EDMAKTUB, Barcelona.

Field Research · Churchill, MB · 2023

Cetacean Bioacoustics: Anthropogenic Disturbance to Churchill River Belugas

Independent field research on anthropogenic disturbance to the Churchill River beluga subpopulation, combining boat-based transect surveys, acoustic monitoring, and photo identification. Analysed 5,642 photos from a 10,239-image catalogue to quantify the frequency and distribution of scarring. Conducted with Beluga Bits, Assiniboine Park Zoo, and Polar Bear International.

Complete Bioacoustics Field Ecology Conservation

Molecular Evolution · December 2023

Purifying Selection in CXCR2 Reveals Differences in Cetacean Rate of Protein Evolution

Independent analysis of CXCR2 protein evolution across 20 cetacean and artiodactyl species, assessing whether purifying selection on this immune receptor contributes to reduced cancer incidence in large-bodied baleen whales. EEB460, University of Toronto.

Complete Bioinformatics

Spatial Analysis · 2020–2021

The Biogeography and Carbon Footprint of Coral Reef Science

Spatial and carbon footprint analysis examining geographic patterns in coral reef science production and research travel. Used MERMAID reef health data integrated with a coral reef publications dataset in R Studio. Darling Lab, University of Toronto.

Complete Conservation R / QGIS

NSERC USRA · 2020

Evolutionary Genomics of C4 Photosynthesis

NSERC Undergraduate Student Research Award-funded independent literature review and research on C4 photosynthesis, covering convergent evolution, Kranz anatomy, gene expression, and the significance of C4 crop research. Sage Lab, University of Toronto.

Methods & tools: I work with bioacoustic analysis software (Raven Lite, Audacity, R/seewave), QGIS for spatial analysis and mapping, and statistical analysis in R (ggplot2, tidyverse). I have experience with PAML for molecular evolution, phylogenetic reconstruction in MEGA and Mr. Bayes, sequence alignment in ClustalO, and tree visualization in FigTree. I work with NCBI and Ensembl for sequence acquisition and BLAST search, Python for large-scale biodiversity data analysis including multi-million record GBIF datasets, and Google Earth Engine for remote sensing and geospatial analysis. My field methods include boat-based transect surveys, photo identification, and systematic classification. I produce scientific illustrations as a working part of my research and write about science for public audiences.
