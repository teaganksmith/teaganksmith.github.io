Science that
listens to the
animal world.

I'm a science writer with two closely linked interests: the science of animal consciousness and language, and the conservation and policy implications of what that science reveals. I'm also the founder of Spectral, a science visualization studio, and the cofounder of Accendra. From a background in comparative physiology and field research with whales, I write about what we're learning about non-human minds, and what we're obligated to do with that knowledge.

Who owns a whale's voice?

A global survey asked 1,057 people across sixty-seven countries who should own the recording of a whale's song. Four in ten said the conservation community; nearly one in five said the whale. The courts are about to face the same question.
